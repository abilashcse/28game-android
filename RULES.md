# Thuruppugulan / 28 — Rules (implemented)

Four players in fixed partnerships (seats **0+2** vs **1+3**). Deal and play are **counter-clockwise**.

## Deck

32 cards: J-9-A-10-K-Q-8-7 in each suit. Points: J=3, 9=2, A=1, 10=1 (total **28**).

## Deal & auction

1. Dealer shuffles; four cards each.
2. If the first speaker’s packet is **0 points** → cancel / reshuffle.
3. First speaker (dealer’s right) must bid **≥14**. Others may raise or pass.
4. Auction ends after **three consecutive passes** once a bid exists.
5. Highest bidder sets a **concealed trump** card from those four.
6. Opponents may **Double**; bidding side may **Redouble** (stake 1→2→4). Bids **≥21** start at stake 2.
7. Dealer deals four more cards (everyone has eight).

## Cancel after full deal

Reshuffle if any player has: **0-point hand**, **all four Jacks**, or **all eight of one suit**.

## Play

### Phase 1 (trump concealed)

- Follow suit if able; highest of led suit wins (trump suit has no power yet).
- Bidder may not **lead** trump unless void of other suits.
- If void of led suit you may discard **or Call Trump** (expose), then must play a trump if you have one.

### Phase 2 (trump exposed)

- Highest trump wins; else highest of led suit.
- If the trick is already trumped and you are void of led suit: you must **overtrump** or discard non-trump (no undertrump).
- If defenders hold **no trump** when it is revealed → cancel.
- If trump never exposed by trick 7, bidder must play it on the last trick.

## Scoring (chaka)

Only the bidding team’s pips move: **+stake** if they make the bid in card points, **−stake** if not.  
If they take **less than half** the bid, the loss is doubled. Match ends at **±6**.

## Thani

Bidder may declare **Thani** (solo) during the auction; auction ends and they choose trump.
